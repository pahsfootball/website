1. A Family-Friendly Environment

PAHS Football and the Fan Zone are designed to be a supportive, fun, and safe environment for fans, students, parents, and athletes of all ages. By creating an account and participating in our community, you agree to treat others with respect and good sportsmanship.

2. Prohibited Content & Behavior

To maintain a safe environment, the following behaviors are strictly prohibited. Engaging in any of these will result in immediate moderation action:

  • Profanity & Vulgarity: The use of explicit language, slurs, or highly offensive terminology.
  • Filter Evasion: Intentionally misspelling, substituting characters (e.g., using numbers for letters), or otherwise attempting to bypass our automated profanity and username filters.
  • Inappropriate Usernames: Usernames must be family-friendly. Names containing suggestive, offensive, or vulgar terminology will result in an immediate ban.
  • Harassment & Bullying: Threatening, targeting, mocking, or attacking players, coaches, officials, or other fans.
  • Spam & Scams: Posting fake ticket links, unauthorized merchandise sales, malicious websites, or repetitive spam messages.

3. Account Suspensions & Bans

PAHS Football administrators reserve the right to moderate the Fan Zone at their sole discretion. We actively monitor posts and user reports.

Violating these Terms and Conditions may result in the immediate removal of your content, a temporary suspension of your posting privileges, or a permanent ban of your account and IP address without prior warning.

You are solely responsible for the content you post in the Fan Zone. PAHS Football is not liable for user-generated content, external links posted by users, or any damages resulting from interactions within the community.

5. Age Requirements

To protect the privacy of children and comply with online privacy laws, you must be at least 13 years of age to create an account or participate in the Fan Zone.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we discover that an account has been created by a user under the age of 13, it will be immediately terminated, and all associated data will be deleted.

Any accounts supplying false information in order to try to get around the age verification will be terminated and associated equipment could be hardware or IP banned as well.
